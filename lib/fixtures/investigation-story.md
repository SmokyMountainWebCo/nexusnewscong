## Surface narrative

Champi was a typhoon east-southeast of Guam in the warnings already on the desk.

## Primary evidence

JTWC warning 10 placed the storm near 12.0N 149.6E, with a Typhoon Warning for Guam and Rota.

> A passage south of Guam would mean less coastal flooding than a direct hit.

Inference: The 175,254 exposure figure is a model, not a headcount.

## Who benefits

Readers who need a short list of what would confirm or weaken the forecast.

## What coverage is leaving out

The filed sources did not confirm that shelters had opened.

## Bottom line / conclusions

The warning is in the record. The impact is not settled.
