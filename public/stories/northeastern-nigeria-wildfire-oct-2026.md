# Satellite alert flags low-impact wildfire in northeastern Nigeria

A wildfire burned about 7,464 hectares, roughly 18,400 acres, in far northeastern Nigeria from Oct. 7 to Oct. 14, according to the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System.

GDACS rated the fire Green, its lowest alert level, based on satellite data from the Global Wildfire Information System. The fire's coordinates place it toward the Lake Chad basin.

No source reviewed for this report identified a cause, casualties or damage, and Nexus News Media found no news coverage of the fire.

Nexus News Media will update this report if more information becomes available.
