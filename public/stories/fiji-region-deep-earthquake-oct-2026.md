# Deep magnitude 5.9 earthquake strikes Fiji region, no tsunami threat

A magnitude 5.9 earthquake struck deep beneath the sea in the Fiji region at 10:49 GMT on Oct. 14, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.

The quake hit at a depth of about 585 kilometers, or 364 miles, the USGS said. The agency gave it a green alert in its PAGER impact system, its lowest level, and did not flag a tsunami threat.

The Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System, or GDACS, reported the same magnitude, time and depth and also rated the quake Green, its lowest alert level.

Nexus News Media found no reports of damage, injuries or people feeling the shaking.
